Passive drone-defence concept

The concept is presented as a passive physical-spacing accessory rather than an electronic counter-UAS device. It does not transmit, jam, spoof, hack, electrify, launch projectiles, or provide instructions for damaging aircraft.

Stand-off geometry

Flexible radial elements increase the physical envelope around the rucksack and illustrate a passive separation concept.

Wearable architecture

An over-pack harness and lightweight frame distribute the prototype across multiple rucksack attachment points.

Modular prototype

Flexible rods, plastic mesh, straps, clips and blunt end caps are arranged as removable prototype components.

Important: Drone and aviation laws vary by jurisdiction. This concept is not represented as approved counter-UAS equipment. Do not use it to intentionally interfere with, capture, damage or bring down an aircraft or drone. Any real-world product would require appropriate engineering, safety, regulatory and legal review.

Design concept & prototype layout

The design below translates the concept image into a simple non-electrified display prototype using flexible hardware-store-type materials.

Concept materials

  • Flexible fiberglass marker/tent-style rods or flexible plastic rod
  • Blunt rubber or plastic end caps
  • Flexible plastic utility mesh
  • Nylon webbing and side-release buckles
  • Reusable plastic ties/clips
  • Flexible plastic tubing for the perimeter support
  • Foam padding at pack contact points

Layout sequence

Over-pack harness
Arrange removable upper, side and lower straps around the existing rucksack.
Support structure
Fit a lightweight flexible perimeter support over the rear of the pack and distribute it across several straps.
Mesh carrier
Loosely attach flexible mesh to the support structure while retaining access to the pack.
Flexible quill layout
Arrange numerous lightweight blunt-ended rods radially around the support so they can flex independently.
Balance check
Keep heavier fittings close to the ruck and ensure the pack can still be removed normally.

Design characteristics

The visual design uses alternating green/black flexible elements with more attachment density near the ruck and lightweight sections extending outward. Outer ends remain separated rather than being rigidly joined together.

Prototype only in a controlled environment with no energized components, sharpened ends or rigid penetrating elements.
